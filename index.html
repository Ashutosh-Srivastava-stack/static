Frequently Asked Questions

How are study materials and notes verified?

Notes uploaded to EduVault undergo review by verified faculty admins and peer moderators to ensure academic integrity and syllabus alignment before being tagged as verified.

Can students contribute their own notes?

Yes! Students can submit their coursework notes to the review queue. Once approved by a faculty admin, notes are added to the vault and credited to the student.

How does the Admin Portal work?

Faculty members signing in with Admin credentials gain access to real-time publishing tools, download analytics, and full security audit trails for all active users.
